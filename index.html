Allah SAW states in the Qur'an e Majeed…

“* اَلَّذِى عَلَّمَ بِالْقَلَمِ * عَلَّمَ الْإِنسَـٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ”

In comprehension from these holy words I have started my business "Al Khattaat", where I showcase my Arabic Calligraphy.

Feel free to contact me for calligraphy pieces, whether it's your name, an ayat from the Qur'an e Majeed, a Hadith of Rasulallah SAW or one of Moulana Ali AS's Kalaam — you are guaranteed to love it!

Even if you have a picture that has captured one of your memorable moments… contact me so that I can elevate it by writing a phrase or a sentence of your choice in Arabic, over the picture so that you can frame it in your room or some place where you are reminded of your exceptional instance.
